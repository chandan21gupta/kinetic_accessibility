# Archived exploratory figures

These figures are excluded from the current analysis presentation.

`peq_vs_pkin_60s.png` and `gap_distribution_60s.png` compare exact ViennaRNA microstate probability with coarse-grained DrTransformer representative population. They do not provide a matched parity comparison and must not be used as evidence of kinetic enrichment or deficits.

`mean_occupancy.png` and `target_heatmap.png` are descriptive kinetic summaries. They do not establish deficits relative to equilibrium and can obscure differences in coverage or coarse-graining across rates.

The current primary figure is `../matched_parity_60s.png`. See `../../results/analysis_report.md` for the supported interpretation and limitations.
